# Brand and mascot concepts: what was explored and why Tibi won

Decided 2026-11-08. References studied for communication only, never copied: Yellow Dude (a character carries every explanation), 3Blue1Brown (one picture that changes, never a list of facts), Kurzgesagt (a recognisable palette and an educational voice), sports-science anatomy plates (x-ray layering).

## Hard constraints from the brief

Not a muscular cartoon human with a yellow body; recognisable at phone size; works for anatomy *and* comedy; supports exercises with plausible joint mechanics; maintainable on the workspace's 2D HyperFrames pipeline (no Blender on the host, 8 GB RAM, one heavy job at a time).

## Why D wins

1. **The brand promise is the character's power.** "See what your body is doing" is literally what Tibi does: the body turns to glass and the working muscles light. Every film's key move (x-ray, scale dive) starts from the mascot, so the mascot is never decoration.
2. **Distinctive silhouette at small sizes:** a big rounded head, a light face patch with two brow lobes, the marigold spark, and orange joint markers. None of these belong to an existing character; the colour is cobalt, not yellow, and the body is athletic but not hulking.
3. **Inclusive and neutral:** a non-human cobalt skin avoids skin-tone and body-type casting, so viewers project themselves onto Tibi.
4. **Biomechanics built in:** the markers are where joint-angle arcs, bar paths and centre-of-mass overlays attach, so the science graphics feel native.
5. **Mood without words:** the spark flares, droops or tilts with emotion and reads even when the face is small or turned away (back view).

Rejected: A cannot do squats or running truthfully (no human joints). B is accurate but cold and generic (every 3D anatomy app has one). C misleads on human anatomy. E is a generic coach with no visual hook, and a human face invites uncanny lip-sync.

## Names considered

Brand: Sinew (chosen: short, physical, scientific, ownable lowercase wordmark), Fibre Lab, Inner Engine, Body Logic, Load & Lift. Mascot: Tibi (chosen: from *tibia*, friendly, two syllables, easy in every language we expect), Myo (taken by a well-known armband product), Sarco, Kilo, Pulse.
