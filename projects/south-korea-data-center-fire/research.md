## Verified claims used in the film

- **When and where:** report received about 8:20 p.m., Friday 26 September 2025; fifth-floor server room, NIRS data center, Daejeon. Fully extinguished the next day.
- **Origin:** a lithium-ion UPS battery that had been disconnected for relocation to the basement. The exact cause was still under investigation as of late September.
- **Scale:** 647 systems housed at NIRS; 96 directly damaged; 551 shut down as a precaution (647 = 96 + 551).
- **Disrupted:** government websites, Korea Post, Government24, the mobile ID app.
- **G-Drive:** in use since 2018; about 858 TB; destroyed; no external backup because its large-capacity, low-performance structure did not allow it (Interior Ministry); head of the digital government innovation office called it "a mistake".
- **Other systems:** backed up daily, on separate equipment in the same center and at a remote facility.
- **Recovery:** 17.3% of 647 on 2 Oct; 89% of 709 on 31 Oct; 98.2% of 709 (696) on 14 Nov, 49 days after. 13 of 16 systems moved to Daegu were still in recovery on 14 Nov.
- **Accountability:** four booked on suspicion of professional negligence (1 Oct); later reports say five charged. Not a finding of guilt.
