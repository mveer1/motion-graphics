## Verified claims used in the film

- **When:** night of 2-3 December 1984. Pressure rose from about 10:30 p.m.; the reaction became critical about 12:40 a.m.; the leak stopped about 2 a.m.
- **City:** Bhopal had 894,539 residents (film: "almost nine hundred thousand").
- **What:** methyl isocyanate (MIC) from tank E610, which held 42 tons. MIC was an intermediate for the pesticide Sevin (carbaryl).
- **How much:** about 30 tonnes in 45-60 minutes; some sources say about 40 tonnes within two hours. Film says "about thirty tonnes ... in under an hour".
- **Who:** Union Carbide India Limited; Union Carbide Corporation held a majority, the remaining 49.1% was Indian public and government-controlled banks.
- **Safeguards:** refrigeration shut down January 1982; vent gas scrubber on standby with insufficient caustic soda and power; flare tower had a pipe removed and was improperly sized; the public siren sounded briefly about 12:50 a.m. and was turned off per company procedure.
- **Exposed:** more than 500,000 people.
- **Settlement and law:** US$470 million in 1989 (upheld 1991). June 2010: seven former UCIL employees convicted of death by negligence, two years each; an eighth died before sentencing. Warren Anderson declared a fugitive 1992, US declined extradition, died 29 September 2014. Supreme Court dismissed a curative petition on 14 March 2023.
- **Present day:** January 2025 shipment of hazardous waste from the plant to a disposal site at Pithampur (about 250 km); most outlets say 337 tonnes, others 307-377. A 2023 report put more than 120,000 people as still chronically ill.

| Question | Positions |

| How many died? | 2,259 immediate official count; **3,787** government-confirmed; 3,000-10,000 in the first days depending on source; **about 23,000** (22,917, survivor-group and ICMR-derived) overall; other estimates over 25,000. ## Deliberately left out

- Railway staff actions that night: accounts differ on details (timings, stations alerted).
- The current state of soil and groundwater at the site: no 2025-2026 source was found; only the January 2025 waste removal is stated.
- Hospital and relief details: not verified in this pass.
- "Crowded neighbourhoods beside the plant": widely reported but not re-verified here, so the film says only "at the edge of the city".
