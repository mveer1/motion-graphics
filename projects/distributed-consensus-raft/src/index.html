The Challenge of Distributed Consensus
Multiple independent servers must agree on a single shared state, even when nodes crash or packets drop
Leader Election & Quorum
Nodes Elect a Single Coordinator
When an election timer expires, a candidate requests votes until winning a majority quorum (N/2 + 1)
Log Replication
Leader Broadcasts Append-Only Log Entries
Client mutations enter the leader and propagate across the cluster via periodic heartbeat messages
Consensus Commitment
Majority Acknowledgement Finalizes the State
Once stored on a majority quorum, the entry is committed permanently and applied to state machines
