# Kokoro Voice Catalog & Styling Guide

Kokoro 0.9.4 includes calibrated voice models. Each voice has an acoustic fingerprint best suited to specific explainer themes:

---

## 1. American English (`lang="a"`)

- **`am_adam` (Default Technical)**
  - *Tone:* Grounded, authoritative, neutral.
  - *Recommended speed:* `0.95`
  - *Best for:* Systems architecture, compiler design, algorithms, machine learning backpropagation.

- **`af_bella` (Conversational & Warm)**
  - *Tone:* Friendly, engaging, expressive.
  - *Recommended speed:* `0.95` – `1.00`
  - *Best for:* Educational introductions, high-level analogies, product demos.

- **`am_michael` (Documentary & Narrative)**
  - *Tone:* Resonant, deliberate, cinematic.
  - *Recommended speed:* `0.90` – `0.95`
  - *Best for:* Astrophysics, black holes, history of computing, epic scientific discoveries.

- **`af_sarah` (Academic & Analytical)**
  - *Tone:* Crisp, precise, didactic.
  - *Recommended speed:* `0.95`
  - *Best for:* Mathematics, algorithmic proofs, data structures.

---

## 2. British English (`lang="b"`)

- **`bm_george` (Scholarly British Male)**
  - *Tone:* Classic British lecture, refined enunciation.
  - *Recommended speed:* `0.95`
  - *Best for:* Physics explainers, formal logic.

- **`bf_emma` (Sophisticated British Female)**
  - *Tone:* Calm, measured, poised.
  - *Recommended speed:* `0.95`
  - *Best for:* Scientific documentaries, bio-informatics.
