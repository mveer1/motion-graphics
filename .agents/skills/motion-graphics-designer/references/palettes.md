# Technical Color Palettes for Motion Graphics

Use these calibrated hex palettes to ensure high contrast, accessibility, and visual hierarchy on 1080p and 720p screens.

---

## 1. Palette A: "Dark Tech" (Default Explainer)
*Best for: Neural networks, machine learning, systems architecture, computer science.*

- **Canvas Background:** `#080c14` (primary dark void)
- **Panel / Surface:** `#0d1117` with border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Input / State A:** `#58a6ff` (electric blue), glow: `rgba(88, 166, 255, 0.35)`
- **Processing / Hidden Layers:** `#e3b341` (amber / warm gold)
- **Target / Output / Success:** `#3fb950` (emerald green)
- **Error / Loss / Gradient:** `#f85149` (crimson) to `#ff9500` (safety orange)
- **Primary Typography:** `#f0f6fc` (headers, title, values)
- **Secondary Typography:** `#8b949e` (labels, axis titles, descriptions)

---

## 2. Palette B: "Cyber Cyan"
*Best for: Networking, cybersecurity, distributed databases, cloud protocols.*

- **Canvas Background:** `#050914`
- **Primary Accent:** `#00f2fe` (neon cyan)
- **Secondary Accent:** `#4facfe` (deep sky blue)
- **Warning / Alert:** `#ff007f` (neon magenta)
- **Neutral Lines:** `rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.15)`
- **Text Primary:** `#ffffff`
- **Text Secondary:** `#7d93b2`

---

## 3. Palette C: "Quantum Violet"
*Best for: Quantum computing, black holes, astrophysics, theoretical physics.*

- **Canvas Background:** `#090714`
- **Primary Particle / Wave:** `#a855f7` (violet)
- **High Energy Accent:** `#ec4899` (electric pink)
- **Cosmic Light Accent:** `#38bdf8` (starlight blue)
- **Grid / Field Lines:** `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.12)`
- **Text Primary:** `#fdf4ff`
- **Text Secondary:** `#a1a1aa`
