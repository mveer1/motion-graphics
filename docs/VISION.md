# Motion Lab: Business Vision

## Purpose

Motion Lab exists to produce our own content: narrated explainers and short-form social videos.

## What we're optimizing for

- **Deliverables:** narrated explainers and short-form social videos.
- **Quality bar:** premium visual craft and clear, accurate explanation are equally important. Neither is traded away for the other.
- **Speed:** fast shipping is not a current priority.

## Subjects

Technology and software, business and finance, science and research, history, mathematics, and news.

## Goals and scale

- **6-month goal:** ship a set number of finished videos. The exact count is not yet decided.
- **Expected cadence:** roughly weekly. This is not finalized.
- **Timeline:** no dated milestones yet.

## What "shipped" means

A video is shipped once it is rendered in the format or formats requested for it. Not every video gets every format. Only the formats asked for are rendered, and the default is portrait.

## Constraints

- **Hardware:** we are staying on the current 8 GB Apple Silicon M2 machine for the foreseeable future. The one-render-at-a-time rule therefore remains a permanent constraint, not a temporary one.

## Non-goals

- No cloud rendering. Everything runs locally on Apple Silicon.
- No live-action footage. Motion graphics only.
- No in-house voice actors. Narration is synthetic (Kokoro).
