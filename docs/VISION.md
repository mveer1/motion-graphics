# Motion Lab: Business Vision

## Purpose

Motion Lab exists to produce our own content: narrated explainers and short-form social videos.

## What we're optimizing for

- **Deliverables:** narrated explainers and short-form social videos, in exactly three types (`specs/video-types.json`), each with a 15 s margin at both ends:
  - **Short-form (micro explainer):** 20-60 s, portrait. Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts. One idea, Hook, Explanation, Payoff.
  - **Mid-form (focused explainer):** 60-180 s, portrait or landscape. YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn. One concept in depth, with a visual demonstration.
  - **Long-form (documentary or deep dive):** 5-12 minutes, landscape (portrait when the brief asks: first used for the upper-body workout film, 2026-11-09). Primarily YouTube. Narrative-driven, told in chapters.
  Each form has its own storytelling, scripting, pacing and editing playbook; subject knowledge (history, technology, science, finance, ...) is separate (`docs/video-forms.md`).
  Any of them can be part of a series, and a series may mix types.
- **Quality bar:** premium visual craft and clear, accurate explanation are equally important. Neither is traded away for the other.
- **Speed:** fast shipping is not a current priority.

## Subjects

Technology and software, business and finance, science and research, history, mathematics, news, and fitness and exercise science (the Sinew brand: strength training for running, gym and bodybuilding, muscle growth and performance).

## Goals and scale

- **6-month goal:** ship a set number of finished videos. The exact count is not yet decided.
- **Expected cadence:** roughly weekly. This is not finalized.
- **Timeline:** no dated milestones yet.

## What "shipped" means

A video is shipped once it is rendered in the format of its type: portrait for a short-form film, portrait or landscape (as the brief chose) for a mid-form film, landscape (or portrait, when its brief asks) for a long-form film. Only that format is rendered.

## Constraints

- **Hardware:** we are staying on the current 8 GB Apple Silicon M2 machine for the foreseeable future. The one-render-at-a-time rule therefore remains a permanent constraint, not a temporary one.

## Non-goals

- No cloud rendering. Everything runs locally on Apple Silicon.
- No live-action footage. Motion graphics only.
- No in-house voice actors. Narration is synthetic (Kokoro).
